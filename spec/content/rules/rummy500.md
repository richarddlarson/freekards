# Rummy 500

Two players. Put sets and runs down on the table to score their points, and pick up cards from anywhere in the discard pile. The first to 500 points wins.

## Melds

- A **set** is 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, like 9♣ 9♦ 9♠.
- A **run** is 3 or more cards of the same suit in order, like 5♦ 6♦ 7♦.
- The **Ace** can be **low or high**: A-2-3 and Q-K-A are both runs, but K-A-2 is not.
- **Laying off** means adding a card to a meld that is already on the table: the fourth card of a set, or the next card at either end of a run. You can lay off on **your own melds or your opponent's**.

## Card values

- Aces: **15**.
- 10s, Jacks, Queens and Kings: **10**.
- 2 to 9: their number.

## The deal

- Each player gets **13 cards**. The next card starts the discard pile face up, and the rest is the face-down **stock**.
- The discard pile is **spread out** so you can see every card in it.
- The computer deals the first hand, so you play first. The deal then alternates.

## Order of play

On your turn:

1. **Draw:** take the top card of the stock, **or** take **any card in the discard pile along with every card above it**.
2. **Meld and lay off** as much as you like: put new sets and runs down on the table, and lay off cards on any meld.
3. **Discard** one card face up to end your turn.

## Taking from the discard pile

- You may take a card from **below the top** only if you can **use it right away**, in a new meld or as a lay-off.
- Your **next** meld or lay-off must use that card, and you can't discard until you have used it.
- If you take just the top card, you can't throw it back on the same turn.

## End of a hand

- The hand ends when a player **goes out** by getting rid of their last card, whether with a meld, a lay-off, or a discard. You don't need to finish with a discard.
- The hand also ends if the **stock runs out**.

## Scoring

- You score the value of **every card you put on the table** this hand, including the cards you laid off on your opponent's melds.
- Then you **subtract** the value of the cards still in your hand. A hand score can be negative.

## Winning

After a hand, if either player has **500 or more**, the higher score wins. If the scores are tied, another hand is played.

## FreeKards choices

- This is the 2-player game, with 13 cards each.
- A game takes about 4 hands, so there is no shorter option.
- **Calling "Rummy!"** (grabbing a discard that your opponent could have played on a meld) isn't part of this version.

## KardCoins

A win earns **80** KardCoins and a loss **10**, multiplied by the difficulty (Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2).

## Playing on this table

- Your hand is grouped into melds and leftover cards. The **Arrange** button switches between Melds, Suit, and Rank.
- **Drawing:** tap the stock or the top discard to take it. Tap a card lower in the pile to take it and everything above it; you'll be asked to confirm.
- **Melding:** tap cards to select them, then tap **Meld** or **Lay off**. A card you must use glows gold until you play it.
- **Discarding:** select one card and tap **Discard**, or drag it onto the discard pile.
- **☰** (or the Esc key) pauses the game. The **?** button opens these rules.
