# Hearts

Four players, each playing for themselves. Try not to win tricks that hold hearts or the Queen of Spades. The lowest score wins.

## The goal

Points are bad in Hearts. Every heart you win costs you **1 point**, and the **Queen of Spades** costs **13**. When a hand ends with anyone at **100 or more**, the game is over and the player with the **lowest** score wins.

## The deal and the pass

- The whole deck is dealt: **13 cards** each.
- Before play, everyone passes **3 cards** at the same time: to the **left** on hand 1, to the **right** on hand 2, **across** the table on hand 3, and **no pass** on hand 4. Then the cycle starts over.
- You can't look at the cards passed to you until you have chosen your own three.

## Order of play

1. Whoever holds the **2♣** leads it to start the first trick.
2. Play goes to the left (clockwise). Each player plays one card.
3. You must **follow suit** (play the suit that was led) if you can. If you can't, you may play any card, except as listed under Special rules.
4. The **highest card of the suit led** wins the trick. Aces are high, and there are no trumps.
5. The winner takes the trick and leads the next one. After 13 tricks the hand is scored.

## Special rules

- **First trick:** you can't play a heart or the Queen of Spades on the first trick, unless you have nothing else you are allowed to play.
- **Leading hearts:** you can't lead a heart until hearts are **broken** (someone has played a heart on an earlier trick), unless you have only hearts left.
- Playing the Queen of Spades doesn't break hearts.

## Scoring

- Each heart you won: **+1**. The Queen of Spades: **+13**.
- **Shooting the moon:** if you win **all 13 hearts and the Queen of Spades** in one hand, you score **0** and every other player gets **+26**.

## Winning

The game ends after a hand in which anyone reaches **100 or more**. The **lowest** score wins. If two or more players tie for the lowest score, more hands are played until one player is lowest on their own.

## Optional rule: Jack of Diamonds

You can turn this on by choosing **Jack of Diamonds −10** on the setup screen. When it is on, the score panel's title shows "J♦ −10".

- Whoever wins the **J♦** takes **10 points off** their score for the hand.
- You don't need the J♦ to shoot the moon. Its −10 still counts for whoever won it, including the player who shot the moon.
- The J♦ may be played on the first trick.

## KardCoins

You earn KardCoins for where you finish: **100** for 1st, **40** for 2nd, **15** for 3rd, and **5** for 4th, multiplied by the difficulty (Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2). Each time you shoot the moon adds **+25**.

## Playing on this table

- **Passing:** tap 3 cards to lift them (tap again to put one back), then tap the **Pass** button.
- **Playing:** on your turn, cards you can't play are dimmed. Tap a card to play it, or drag it up onto the table. Your **Card response** setting decides how quickly a touch plays a card.
- **☰** (or the Esc key) pauses the game. The **?** button opens these rules.
