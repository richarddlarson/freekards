# Five-Card Draw

Poker for you and three regulars from the card room, played with your own KardCoins as chips. Everyone gets five cards, bets, swaps cards once, and bets again. The best hand at the end wins the pot.

## Hands, best to worst

1. **Straight flush:** five cards in order, all one suit, like 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ 8♥ 9♥. The best is a **royal flush**, 10 to Ace.
2. **Four of a kind:** four cards of one rank.
3. **Full house:** three of one rank and two of another, like K K K 4 4.
4. **Flush:** five cards of one suit.
5. **Straight:** five cards in order. The Ace can be high (10 J Q K A) or low (A 2 3 4 5).
6. **Three of a kind**.
7. **Two pair**.
8. **One pair**.
9. **High card:** none of the above; your highest card counts.

When two hands are the same kind, the higher cards win: a pair of Kings beats a pair of Queens, and if the pairs match, the next-highest cards decide. Suits never break a tie: equal hands **split the pot**.

## Sitting down

- Choose the **stakes**. Your chips come out of your KardCoins: the buy-in, or everything you have if that's less (but at least the minimum).
  - **Low:** ante 5, bets of 10 and 20. Buy-in 200 (at least 50).
  - **Medium:** ante 10, bets of 20 and 40. Buy-in 500 (at least 100).
  - **High:** ante 25, bets of 50 and 100. Buy-in 1,000 (at least 250).
- The regulars bring their own KardCoins too. Someone without enough can't sit down.

## Order of play

1. Everyone puts in the **ante**. The **dealer button** moves one seat to the left every hand.
2. Everyone gets **5 cards**.
3. **First betting round**, starting left of the dealer. With no bet yet you may **check** (pass) or **bet**. Once someone bets, you may **fold** (give up the hand), **call** (match the bet), or **raise** (match it and add one more bet).
4. **The draw:** everyone still in throws away any number of cards, **0 to 5**, and gets new ones. Keeping all five is **standing pat**.
5. **Second betting round**, with bets twice as big.
6. **Showdown:** everyone still in shows their hand, and the best hand wins the pot.

If everyone else folds, the last player left wins the pot without showing their cards.

## Betting

- Every bet and raise is a fixed size: the small bet before the draw, the big bet after it.
- Each round allows **a bet and 3 raises**, no more.
- If you can't afford to call, you can still call with everything you have (**all in**). You can only win as much from each player as you put in yourself; the rest goes to a **side pot** for the others.

## Winning

There's no final score in poker: you win **chips**. Play as many hands as you like, and leave the table whenever you want from the ☰ menu. If you run out of chips you can buy more from your KardCoins, or leave.

## FreeKards choices

- **No openers:** you don't need a pair of Jacks to bet first.
- You may draw up to **5 cards**.
- No wild cards.
- If a regular runs out of chips, another regular may take the empty seat.

## KardCoins

Your chips are your KardCoins. When you leave the table, every chip in front of you goes back to your balance, so whatever you won or lost is your result. If the app closes in the middle of a game, your chips from the last finished hand are returned.

## Playing on this table

- Each player's chips are stacked beside them, with the amount below. Bets sit in front of each player until the round ends, then slide into the **pot** in the middle.
- **Betting:** use the buttons above your cards: **Fold**, **Check** or **Call**, and **Bet** or **Raise**. Each button shows how many chips it puts in.
- **Drawing:** tap the cards you want to throw away (they lift up), then tap **Draw**. Tap **Stand pat** to keep all five.
- The **D** chip shows the dealer. The name of your hand is shown above your cards.
- **☰** (or the Esc key) pauses the game and has **Leave table**. The **?** button opens these rules.
