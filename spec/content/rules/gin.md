# Gin Rummy

Two players. Draw and discard to build your hand into sets and runs, then knock to end the hand before your opponent does. The first to 100 points wins.

## Melds and deadwood

- A **set** is 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, like 7♠ 7♥ 7♦.
- A **run** is 3 or more cards of the same suit in order, like 4♥ 5♥ 6♥.
- In Gin Rummy the **Ace is always low**: A-2-3 is a run, but Q-K-A is not.
- Cards that aren't in a meld are **deadwood**. Deadwood counts against you: Aces **1**, 2 to 10 their number, Jacks, Queens and Kings **10**.

## The deal

- Each player gets **10 cards**. The next card is turned face up to start the discard pile (the **upcard**), and the rest is the face-down **stock**.
- The computer deals the first hand, so you play first. The deal then alternates.

## The first turn

1. The player who didn't deal may **take the upcard** or **pass**.
2. If they pass, the dealer may take it or pass.
3. If both pass, the player who didn't deal draws from the stock.

## Order of play

On your turn:

1. **Draw** one card: the top card of the stock, or the top card of the discard pile.
2. **Discard** one card face up. You can't throw back the card you just took from the discard pile.
3. Instead of an ordinary discard, you may **knock** if your deadwood after discarding is **10 or less**. Knocking with no deadwood at all is going **gin**.

## When someone knocks

- Both hands are laid face up, arranged into melds.
- After a knock (but not after gin), the other player may **lay off** deadwood on the knocker's melds, such as the fourth 7 on a set of 7s, or the next card at either end of a run.
- **Knock:** if the other player's deadwood is higher, the knocker scores the difference.
- **Undercut:** if the other player's deadwood is equal or lower, they score the difference **+ 25**.
- **Gin:** the player who went gin scores all of the other player's deadwood **+ 25**. No laying off.

## Dead hand

If a discard leaves only **2 cards** in the stock and nobody has knocked, the hand is **dead**: nobody scores, and the next hand is dealt.

## Winning

The game ends after the hand in which a player reaches **100 points**, and that player wins. Then the bonuses are added to the final totals:

- **+25** for each hand a player won;
- **+100** for winning the game;
- the winner's total is **doubled** if the other player didn't win a single hand (a **shutout**).

## FreeKards choices

- Melds, deadwood, and lay-offs are always worked out for you, in whichever way leaves the least deadwood.
- The hand is dead with 2 cards left in the stock.
- **Big Gin** and **Oklahoma Gin** aren't offered.

## KardCoins

A win earns **80** KardCoins and a loss **10**, multiplied by the difficulty (Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2). Each time you go gin adds **+25**.

## Playing on this table

- Your hand is grouped into melds and deadwood. The **Arrange** button switches between Melds, Suit, and Rank.
- **Drawing:** tap the stock or the top discard (at the start of a hand, use the **Take** or **Pass** buttons for the upcard).
- **Discarding:** tap a card to select it, then tap **Discard**, or drag it onto the discard pile. When you can knock, the **Knock** button (or **Gin!**) lights up for a card that allows it.
- **☰** (or the Esc key) pauses the game. The **?** button opens these rules.
