# Spades

Four players in two teams of partners. Each team bids how many tricks it will win, then tries to win exactly that many. Spades are always trump.

## Teams

- You and your **partner** sit across from each other (your partner is at the top of the table). The two players at the sides are the other team.
- Partners' tricks count together, and the team shares one score.

## The deal

- The whole deck is dealt: **13 cards** each.
- **You deal the first hand**, so you see the other three bids before you make yours. The deal moves one seat to the left each hand.

## Bidding

1. Starting with the player to the dealer's left and going around once, each player bids the number of tricks they expect to win, from **1 to 13**, or **Nil**.
2. Your team's **contract** is your bid plus your partner's (a Nil bid adds nothing).
3. **Nil** means you promise to win **no tricks at all**. Your partner still tries to make their own bid.

## Order of play

1. The player to the dealer's left leads the first trick.
2. Play goes to the left (clockwise). Each player plays one card.
3. You must **follow suit** if you can. If you can't, you may play any card, including a spade (that's called **trumping**).
4. The trick is won by the **highest spade** in it, or, if there is no spade, by the **highest card of the suit led**. Aces are high.
5. The winner leads the next trick.
6. **Spades can't be led until they are broken** (a spade has been played on an earlier trick), unless the leader has only spades left.

## Scoring

- **Made your contract** (the team won at least as many tricks as it bid): **+10 per trick bid**, plus **+1 for each extra trick**. Each extra trick is also a **bag**.
- **Set** (the team won fewer tricks than it bid): **−10 per trick bid**.
- **Nil:** **+100** if the Nil bidder wins no tricks, **−100** if they win any. Tricks a Nil bidder wins don't help their partner's bid; each counts +1 and a bag.
- **Bags:** every time a team has collected **10 bags**, it loses **100 points** and 10 bags come off its count.

Example: you bid 4 and your partner bids 3, a contract of 7. Together you win 9 tricks: 70 + 2 = **72 points**, and 2 bags.

## Winning

After a hand, the game is over when a team has reached the target (**300** or **500**, chosen on the setup screen) or has fallen to **−200 or below**. The team with the higher score wins. If the scores are tied, another hand is played.

## FreeKards choices

- The game is to **300** by default, which takes about as long as a game of Hearts. Choose **500** on the setup screen for the full-length game.
- Nil is always allowed. **Blind Nil** (bidding Nil before looking at your cards) isn't offered.
- There is no minimum bid for a team.

## KardCoins

A win earns **80** KardCoins and a loss **10**, multiplied by the difficulty (Easy ×1, Medium ×1.5, Hard ×2). Each Nil you make adds **+25**.

## Playing on this table

- **Bidding:** on your turn the bid panel shows everyone's bids so far and a suggested bid. Tap a number (or **Nil**), then tap **Bid** to confirm.
- **Playing:** each player's badge shows their bid and the tricks they have won. On your turn, cards you can't play are dimmed. Tap a card to play it, or drag it up onto the table.
- **☰** (or the Esc key) pauses the game. The **?** button opens these rules.
